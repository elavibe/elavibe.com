Privacy Policy for Qu

Last updated: July 12, 2026

Qu ("the app," "we," "our") is a personal media queue app that lets you collect podcasts, YouTube videos, and articles into a single playlist for listening and watching, including while driving via Apple CarPlay. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how.

In short: Qu does not collect, track, or sell your personal information. We have no user accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

Information Stored on Your Device

Your queue — the list of podcasts, videos, and articles you add — is stored locally on your device. This information stays on your device and is not transmitted to us or anyone else unless you explicitly choose to share a queue (see below).

Shared Queues

If you use the "Share Queue" feature, the contents of that queue (such as item titles and links) are uploaded to our hosting provider (Supabase) and assigned a short share code. Anyone who has that code can retrieve the shared queue. Shared queues do not contain any personal information about you — only the media items you chose to include. Do not share a queue if it contains links you consider private.

YouTube Sign-In

Qu lets you view YouTube content through an in-app web view that loads YouTube's own website. If you choose to sign in to your Google or YouTube account, that sign-in happens directly with Google through their standard web sign-in page. Qu does not see, collect, or store your Google password or account credentials. Your login session is managed by the system web view's standard cookie storage on your device. Your use of YouTube through the app is subject to Google's and YouTube's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Third-Party Services

To provide search and playback, the app sends requests directly from your device to the following third-party services:

  • Apple iTunes Search API — to search for podcasts.
  • YouTube Data API (Google) — to search for videos and retrieve titles.
  • Podcast RSS feeds — to load episode lists from the podcast publishers you select.
  • Supabase — only when you share or import a queue.

These requests are made to provide the app's core features. Each of these services has its own privacy practices, which are outside our control.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not collect analytics or usage tracking.
  • We do not use advertising or ad networks.
  • We do not require or offer user accounts within Qu.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information.

Children's Privacy

Qu is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
